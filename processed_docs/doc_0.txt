✨ Section 1: Welcoming a New Journey

DITTA:
Welcome, beautiful soul.
Before we dive into knowledge, I want to remind you: this is your journey. Your body, your baby, your rhythm. This is not about perfection — this is about presence.
So, before we begin today’s session, I invite you to place your hand over your womb, take a deep breath in… and just notice what’s here. Any emotions, thoughts, tensions — they’re welcome too.

Pregnancy isn’t just about growing a baby. It’s about growing into the version of you that can hold that baby — with grace, with power, with softness.
If you’re here, it means you care. And that is already enough.

🌱 Section 2: First Trimester – Understanding What’s Happening

DITTA:
In the first trimester, your body is undergoing massive shifts — hormonally, emotionally, even energetically.

Many women feel surprised by the fatigue, the nausea, the heightened sensitivity.
And society often tells us to “just push through” or “keep going.” But that’s not what your body needs.

Instead of powering through, can you pause and listen? Can you give yourself permission to slow down?

> Journal Prompt:
> “What does my body need from me today, and what belief is stopping me from giving it?”

DITTA (teaching):
The hormone hCG rises rapidly during these weeks. That’s what triggers nausea and exhaustion. Your sense of smell may be heightened. Your dreams may become vivid. This is your nervous system responding to the shift in identity.

You are not who you were — and you are not yet who you are becoming.
You’re in the sacred in-between.

🛑 Section 3: Grounding Through Emotional Waves

DITTA:
Emotions during pregnancy can feel like storms. One minute, you’re crying over spilled tea. The next, you’re laughing for no reason.
This doesn’t mean something is wrong. It means you are deeply alive.

When we talk about “mood swings,” we often talk about them like they’re a problem. But what if they’re invitations?

> Practice:
> Place your hand on your chest and whisper:
> “I allow myself to feel, without judgment.”
> “My emotions are valid.”
> “I am safe in my body.”

If you’ve experienced trauma before, these feelings might feel overwhelming. That’s okay. Let the companion hold you in those moments. You can return to this breath. You can return to your rhythm.

🧠 Section 4: Understanding the Nervous System (Trauma-Informed)

DITTA (teaching):
Let’s speak about your nervous system. During pregnancy, it’s not just your body that expands — your emotional capacity does too.

If you have a history of anxiety, disconnection, or chronic over-functioning… pregnancy will bring those patterns to the surface.

That’s not a failure. That’s an invitation.
Your baby wants to grow in safety. And that starts with your safety in your own system.

> Tool: The Voo Sound
> Sit comfortably. Inhale slowly…
> And as you exhale, make a deep “Voooooo” sound.
> This vibrates your vagus nerve and helps you regulate your nervous system.

Repeat three times. Notice the shift.

💬 Section 5: Affirmations to Rewire Safety

> Daily Affirmations:
> - My body knows exactly what to do.
> - I release fear and welcome trust.
> - I am creating life with love.
> - I soften into the unknown.
> - Every sensation is guiding me toward birth.

DITTA:
Affirmations are not about forcing positivity. They are about gently replacing the voice of fear with the voice of love.
Say them when you brush your teeth.
Say them when you wake up in the middle of the night.
Let them re-pattern the inner space your child is growing in.

🤍 Section 6: If You’ve Experienced Loss Before…

DITTA (soft tone):
If you’ve been through loss… I want to say, I see you.

Grief and joy often dance closely during pregnancy after loss. One moment you may feel excited. The next, terrified to get too close, too attached, too hopeful.

That’s valid.

You don’t need to “stay positive.” You need to stay present.

> Ritual:
> Light a candle.
> Name your fear. Speak it aloud.
> Then, whisper to your womb:
> “You are safe now. I’m with you. I won’t abandon you.”

🩺 Section 7: Navigating Doctor Visits

DITTA (practical):
Doctors are there to support you medically. But remember — they are not the authority on your intuition.

Take space before appointments to tune in.

> Checklist Before Appointments:
> - How am I feeling physically today?
> - Is there anything that’s been bothering me?
> - What question do I need to ask, even if it feels silly?
> - What’s one boundary I want to hold?

You are allowed to say “I need more time.” You are allowed to say “No thank you.”
Medical care should be collaborative, not directive.

🤰 Section 8: Connecting With Baby – A Guided Visualization

DITTA (soft, guiding tone):
Close your eyes. Breathe gently.

Picture a soft golden light in your womb space.
With each breath, see it growing brighter.
Now imagine your baby — not as a formed body, but as a soul.
They are floating, surrounded by warmth.
You don’t need to say anything. Just feel their presence.

> Whisper:
> “I’m here with you.”
> “We are growing together.”
> “You chose me, and I choose you.”

When you’re ready, gently return to the room. Wiggle your fingers. Smile. Say thank you.

💡 Section 9: Real Talk – When You’re Overwhelmed

DITTA:
Some days, you’ll forget all of this.
You’ll snap at your partner. You’ll hate your changing body. You’ll feel guilty for feeling anything but joy.

That’s human.

Let the companion remind you:
You’re not doing this wrong. You’re doing this real.

> Emergency Reminder:
> - Drink water.
> - Step outside.
> - Put your hand on your belly and say:
> “This is hard. And I’m still doing it.”

📦 Section 10: What to Expect Next

DITTA (teaching):
In the next phase of your pregnancy, you’ll begin to feel your baby move. You may notice your breath getting shorter. Your hips may ache.

These are signs of expansion.

Your body is preparing.
Your heart is widening.
Your future is growing inside you.

Let’s take it one breath at a time.
